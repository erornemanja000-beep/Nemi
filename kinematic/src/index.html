SC.06 The grade

Shot flat. Finished in the grade.

Our cameras record a flat LOG image that keeps detail in the brightest highlights and the deepest shadows. The look is built afterwards, in our grading suite. Drag the line, then try one of our looks.

SC.07 Script to screen

From script to screen

  1. 01

    Development

    Weeks 1–2

    Treatment, script, mood film and storyboard. We agree on the look before anyone books a camera.

  2. 02

    Pre-production

    Weeks 2–4

    Casting, locations, permits and crew. Every hour of the shoot gets a line in the shot list.

  3. 03

    Production

    1 to 5 shoot days

    A tight crew with cinema cameras, anamorphic lenses and our own lighting package.

  4. 04

    Post-production

    Weeks 4–7

    Edit, sound design, clean-up and the grade, delivered in every format you need.
