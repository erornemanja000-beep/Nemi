# Gradnja lokala, Kling multi-shot (4 kadra po 5 s)

Slike (16:9) u `frames/multishot/`, redosled po dogovoru:
1_zid → 2_izlog_prazan → 3_prozor_roze → 4_lokal_pun → 5_lokal_dons
Kadar N: start = slika N, end = slika N+1. Svaki kadar 5 s, 16:9.

Zajednički stil (već je u svakom promptu): magično ali fotorealistično, zlatni sat,
nežno roze/pistać, zlatne iskrice, kamera mirna, zgrada se ne menja.

## Kadar 1 (1_zid → 2_izlog_prazan)

```
Magical photorealistic ice cream shop commercial, warm golden hour sunlight, blush pink tones, soft golden sparkles. Locked camera, straight frontal view of the same pink building, the facade, gutter and upper windows never change. Starts exactly on the start image and ends exactly on the end image.
0-1s: the closed cream panel glows softly from inside.
1-2.5s: the panel melts like vanilla ice cream, sliding down in thick creamy drips and disappearing into the ground, revealing the empty room behind it.
2.5-4s: clear glass grows across the opening like sugar glass crystallizing with a soft shimmer, white frames draw themselves along its edges and a tall glass door forms on the left and swings open; inside, an empty white gelato display rises up from the floor like a soap bubble taking shape.
4-5s: everything settles exactly into the end image, sparkles fade.
Avoid: people, hands, workers, tools, construction site, flying debris, explosion, different building, extra windows, signs, text, logos, ice cream in the display, cartoon, CGI look, morphing building, warped lines, flicker, camera movement, jump cuts, blurry.
```

## Kadar 3 (3_prozor_roze → 4_lokal_pun)

```
Magical photorealistic ice cream shop commercial, warm golden hour sunlight, blush pink and pistachio green tones, soft golden sparkles. Locked camera, straight frontal view of the same pink building, the facade, gutter and upper windows never change. Starts exactly on the start image and ends exactly on the end image.
0-1s: the rose glass bursts softly into thousands of pink sugar sparkles that float away like petals, the old window frames fade.
1-2.5s: the new storefront assembles itself: white frames slide into place, the big glass pane appears with a bright reflection, the glass door swings open on the left; inside, ceiling lights switch on one by one like little fireflies landing.
2.5-4s: three white menu boards flutter in like paper butterflies and land on the back wall, the drinks fridge glides in from the right, the COFFEE TIME sign lights up, colorful scoops of gelato fly in like a small flock and land in the display one after another.
4-5s: a ribbon of green and pink light paints the DON'S eisdiele logo with the french bulldog onto the front of the display, everything settles exactly into the end image.
Avoid: people, customers, hands, real butterflies, birds, insects, creatures, mess, different shop layout, extra furniture, misspelled text, random logos, cartoon, CGI look, morphing building, warped lines, flicker, camera movement, jump cuts, blurry.
```
