Small roastery, café up front.

POUR / PLAY is a small roastery with a café in Vienna's 7th district. Our roaster sits right behind the counter, so you can watch the beans turn brown while your coffee cools. In Vienna you stay as long as your cup lasts, often longer. We're fine with that. On every bag you'll find the farm, the altitude, the process and the day we roasted it.

9b · BREW GUIDE · 3 koraka = 3 poglavlja

Three steps, three minutes3 steps, 3 minutes

Pour-over for one big cup: 18 g coffee, 288 g water (1:16), 93 °C.

You need: Dripper, paper filter, scale, kettle, timer.
01 · GRIND
[SLIKA: kadar iz GRIND sekvence]

Grind

Grind 18 g of coffee medium-fine, about like table salt. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and pour that water away.

→ Burr Grinder
02 · POUR
[SLIKA: kadar iz POUR sekvence]

Pour

Start the timer. Pour 50 g of water at 93 °C and let it bloom for 45 seconds. Then pour slowly in circles until the scale shows 288 g.

→ Ceramic Dripper · Gooseneck Kettle
03 · SIP
[SLIKA: kadar iz SIP sekvence]

Sip

Let it drip through. At about 3:00 it's done. Swirl the carafe once and pour.

→ Find your cup
Tip: Too sour? Grind finer. Too bitter? Grind coarser. Works well with Guji, Nyeri and Tarrazú Honey. Shop filter coffee
